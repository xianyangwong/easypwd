Remember one passphrase. Get the same strong password for every site, on any device.

EasyPwd calculates a unique password for each website from your email, your master passphrase, and the site's name. There is nothing to sync: install EasyPwd on another computer, enter the same email and passphrase, and you get the same passwords. Passwords you can't change are kept in a local encrypted vault.

No account. No server. No cloud sync. No analytics. No access to the websites you visit.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your email (or any name) and a master passphrase.
2. Type a website, such as github.com, and press Enter.
3. Copy your password. It is calculated, not stored, and it will be the same tomorrow and on every other device.

FEATURES
• Generated passwords: strong, unique, and the same on every device.
• Password versions: if a site is breached, click "New password" to move to the next version. The previous password stays visible until you've updated the site.
• Per-site rules: set the length (8–64) and character types for sites with limits.
• Key fingerprint: four words confirm you typed the same email and passphrase on every device. Different words mean a typo.
• Saved logins: store existing passwords, PINs, and Wi-Fi keys in an AES-256-GCM encrypted vault.
• Usernames, websites, and notes for every login, with one-click copy.
• Random password generator with a strength estimate.
• Auto-lock after 1, 5, 15, or 30 minutes of inactivity.
• Encrypted backups, restore, and import from Chrome's password CSV export.
• Light and dark themes.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• Everything runs inside the extension. It makes no network requests.
• Only two permissions: "storage" for the encrypted vault and "clipboardWrite" to copy a password when you click copy.
• Built on the browser's native Web Crypto: PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 iterations), HKDF, HMAC-SHA-256, and AES-256-GCM.
• The password algorithm is published with test vectors, so anyone can verify or reimplement it.

IMPORTANT
• Your passphrase cannot be recovered. Choose a long one you will remember, such as several unrelated words.
• Anyone who learns your passphrase and email can calculate your generated passwords. Keep it private and unique.
• EasyPwd is open source and has not been independently audited.
