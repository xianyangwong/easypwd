EasyPwd

Privacy policy

EasyPwd does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.

  • Your email or name, master passphrase, logins, and settings stay on your device. Saved logins are encrypted in Chrome's local extension storage. The email or name is stored unencrypted so the lock screen can show it.
  • Generated passwords are calculated on your device and are never stored.
  • The extension makes no network requests. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or servers, and no access to the websites you visit.
  • Backups are files that you export and keep yourself.
  • Uninstalling the extension or using Forgot passphrase? deletes the local vault.

This website has no cookies or analytics. The demo runs entirely in your browser.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
